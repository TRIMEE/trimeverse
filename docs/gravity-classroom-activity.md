# Classroom activity: Same mass, different object

**Question:** If the Sun were replaced by a one-solar-mass black hole, would Earth immediately fall in?

1. Open the [gravity sandbox](https://trimeverse.trimemvp.chatgpt.site/gravity.html). Reset the lab and keep the central mass at one solar mass.
2. Predict what will happen to Earth's orbit when you change the central object from **Star** to **Black hole**. Record one reason.
3. Run the comparison at the same scale and playback speed. Use the orbit trail and the Earth distance and speed readouts. If needed, reset and repeat with the original star.
4. Explain why keeping mass and initial conditions the same keeps the modeled gravity at Earth's distance the same. Then choose a different central mass and describe how the comparison changes.

**What to look for:** In this model, Earth's orbit stays broadly similar when the mass and initial conditions stay the same. A black hole with the same mass does not exert a stronger Newtonian gravitational pull at Earth's distance. Replacing the Sun would drastically change the light and heat Earth receives; this lab does not model those effects or strong gravity near the event horizon.

**Extension:** Add a second massive object and observe how the star moves, too. Ask why the result is different from replacing the central object while keeping its mass fixed.
